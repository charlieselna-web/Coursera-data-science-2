Basic overview

What Glean does

Glean is the Work AI platform that connects enterprise knowledge to search, answers, agents, and the tools teams already use.

1. Finds the right answer

Employees search across connected systems and get grounded, source-backed answers instead of hunting through files, chats, tickets, and docs.

2. Understands enterprise context

Glean builds a permission-aware knowledge graph across content, people, projects, apps, and activity so AI can reason over how work actually happens.

3. Powers governed agents

Teams can automate repeatable workflows with agents that use trusted company context, follow access controls, and work across the enterprise stack.

Token efficiency and developer workflows

Context quality changes the economics of AI work

As AI work moves from single prompts to multi-step workflows, better retrieval becomes more than a UX issue. It affects quality, latency, and token cost.

Glean-published MCP benchmark

In a benchmark of roughly 175 Claude Cowork-style queries, Glean’s centralized index and knowledge graph was preferred about 2.5x as often as off-the-shelf MCP tools. The off-the-shelf approach used 30% more tokens on average.

Why this matters for a GPT/OpenAI strategy
